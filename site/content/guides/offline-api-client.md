## About offline API testing

The HITOP Mac app, iOS app and CLI work with no internet connection, making them a good fit for testing APIs in secure environments, on airplanes, or anywhere without reliable connectivity. The web app needs a connection, because it sends requests to public APIs through the hitop.rws8.tech proxy. All your collections and history are stored on your device.

## Offline capabilities

-   Mac app, iOS app and CLI run fully without an internet connection
-   Test local development APIs (localhost) and machines on your local network
-   Suited to secure or isolated environments
-   No dependency on external services
-   Collections and history stored on your device
-   Work on planes, trains, or in remote locations
