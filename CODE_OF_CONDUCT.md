# Code of conduct

HITOP follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment,
insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests or anywhere the
project is represented.

Report a problem privately to bohdaq@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer
may remove content and block people who break these rules.
